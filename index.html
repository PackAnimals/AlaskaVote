What makes this race unusual?

Peltola is significantly more supportive of oil development, gun ownership and border security than a typical national Democratic candidate. Incumbent Sullivan combines conventional Republican economics with a willingness to support some bipartisan Alaska-focused measures, including the 2021 infrastructure law. The two lesser-known finalists sit on opposite sides of the major contenders: Leslie to Peltola's left and Daniel Sullivan Jr. as an anti-establishment alternative on the right.

Ranked-choice general election

If all four remain on the November ballot, voters rank candidates. If nobody wins a majority of first choices, the lowest candidate is eliminated and those ballots transfer to the voter's next ranked candidate. That makes second-choice support potentially important, especially for voters backing Leslie or Daniel Sullivan Jr.
